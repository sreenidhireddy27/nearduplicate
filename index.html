Locality Sensitive Hashing (LSH) Banding Visualization

b = 16 bands, r = 4 rows/band

Signatures are divided into b bands of r rows. If any band matches identically, documents hash to the same bucket and become a Candidate Pair, bypassing $O(n^2)$ full comparisons!

2. K-Shingling (n-grams)

Transforms sequential text documents into mathematical sets of contiguous word sequences to capture local phrasing.

3. Polynomial Rolling Hashing

Maps high-dimensional variable-length string tokens to fixed 32-bit integer indices using Horner's polynomial rule.

Read DSA Breakdown →
📊

4. Jaccard Similarity

Measures the exact overlap ratio between two distinct sets: the cardinality of their intersection divided by their union.

Read DSA Breakdown →
🧬

5. MinHash Signatures

Generates compact signature vectors of size $m$ whose collision probability mathematically equals the exact Jaccard similarity.

Read DSA Breakdown →
🪣

6. Locality Sensitive Hashing (LSH)

Slices signature vectors into bands and hashes rows to buckets, providing sub-quadratic candidate generation.
